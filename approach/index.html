Our Approach

Designed for Lasting Learning Outcomes

The model

How a programme comes together

  1. 01

    Design

    ALFA BLUE develops the programme model with certified curriculum providers.

  2. 02

    Partner

    ALFA BLUE connects the programme with established local NGOs and schools.

  3. 03

    Support

    Sponsors fund the delivering NGO directly, under their own agreement.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    The NGO delivers the programme to students and reports to its sponsors.

Quality

Programme Quality Standards

Certified curricula

Built on curricula and platforms certified by recognised international education bodies.

Age-appropriate design

Content structured by age group and skill level, from first concepts to advanced projects.

Built-in assessment tools

Progress assessments that delivery partners can use to track learning.

Educator enablement

Delivery partners' educators receive programme guides and onboarding from the curriculum provider.

Monitoring and reporting on programme results are the responsibility of the delivering NGO, in line with its agreements with its sponsors.
