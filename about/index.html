About ALFA BLUE

Strategic advice and partnership development for education

ALFA BLUE Management Consulting is a commercially operated company. We combine consulting experience with a commitment to inclusive education and long-term social benefit.

Our vision

To build a brighter, more inclusive future by preparing the next generation with the digital skills and financial literacy they need to thrive.

Every young learner should have the confidence to code, create, and take part fully in tomorrow's economy.

Our role

Connecting the people who make programmes work

We advise on and develop education programmes, coordinate between curriculum providers and delivery organisations, and help open access to new markets and communities.

We are not a registered NGO or charitable foundation. Our commercial model allows us to work flexibly with non-profit, public and private partners alike.
