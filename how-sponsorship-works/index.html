Sponsorship

How Sponsorship Works

We help companies and foundations support digital and financial literacy for young people by connecting them with established NGO partners and ready-to-deliver programmes.

How it works

Three steps from introduction to delivery

  1. 01

    Connect

    ALFA BLUE presents suitable programmes and introduces the sponsor to a vetted NGO delivery partner.

  2. 02

    Agree

    The sponsor and the NGO agree the programme scope and funding directly between themselves.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    The NGO delivers the programme to students and reports to the sponsor.

Our role

What ALFA BLUE provides

  • Programme design and curriculum partnerships
  • Identification of vetted NGO delivery partners
  • Alignment of the programme with the sponsor's CSR objectives
  • Coordination between all parties

What ALFA BLUE does not do

We do not receive, hold or manage donations or sponsorship funds, and we do not deliver programmes directly to students.
