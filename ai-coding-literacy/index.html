Initiative 01

AI, computer coding and digital literacy for every student

Digital skills increasingly shape access to opportunity. We help make high-quality learning available to students who would otherwise be left out.

Why it matters

More than learning to code

Young people are growing up alongside AI. They need to understand it, question it and create with it.

The digital learning gap

Access to digital learning is still very unequal. Children in low-income countries and rural areas are the least likely to learn the coding and AI skills that increasingly shape opportunity.

2 in 3

school-age children worldwide (1.3 billion aged 3–17) have no internet connection at home. In low-income countries, fewer than 1 in 20 do, compared with nearly 9 in 10 in high-income countries.

UNICEF & ITU, 2020
40%

of primary schools worldwide are connected to the internet, rising to 50% of lower-secondary and 65% of upper-secondary schools.

UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report, 2023
23% vs 94%

of people use the internet in low-income countries, compared with high-income countries. Of the 2.2 billion people still offline, 96% live in low- and middle-income countries.

2 in 3

Closing this gap is why we bring certified, high-quality coding and AI education to communities that would otherwise be left out.

  1. 01

    AI literacy

    Understanding what AI systems do, where they fall short, and how to use them responsibly and critically.

  2. 02

    Computer coding

    Learning to build working programs, from visual block coding through to text-based languages.

  3. 03

    Computational thinking

    Breaking problems into steps, recognising patterns and reasoning logically, skills that transfer across subjects.

  4. 04

    Creativity

    Designing games, animations and digital projects that turn students from consumers into makers.

  5. 05

    Digital citizenship

    Safe, ethical and informed participation online, including privacy and wellbeing.

  6. 06

    Future readiness

    Foundations that open doors to further study and careers in an increasingly digital economy.

Implementation

How a programme can be delivered

Within schools

Integrated into the timetable or offered as after-school enrichment, taught by trained educators using a certified curriculum, with progress tracked on the learning platform.

Through NGOs

Community programmes that reach students outside formal schooling, delivered by trusted local organisations with our coordination and quality oversight.

Sponsored cohorts

CSR- and foundation-funded places for students from communities with limited access, with funding agreed directly with the delivering partner.

Online and blended learning

Live online classes and blended formats extend programmes to rural and remote areas where specialist teachers are scarce.

Global reach

Our curriculum partner's programmes are delivered in more than 30 countries across five continents. Each programme is adapted to the local language, context and school calendar, so proven content can be introduced quickly and responsibly in new markets.

Expertise and partners

Programmes are designed and reviewed with education specialists, including experts holding doctoral degrees in education, pedagogy and learning technology. We seek to work with established international and local NGOs, foundations and education authorities to reach the students who need it most.
