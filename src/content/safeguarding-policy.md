## 1. Policy Statement and ALFA BLUE's Role

**1.1** ALFA BLUE Management Consulting LLC ("ALFA BLUE") develops AI, coding, digital literacy and financial literacy programme models for school-age children. It connects curriculum providers, NGOs and corporate or philanthropic sponsors so that these programmes can be funded and delivered.

**1.2** ALFA BLUE's role is that of a facilitator and coordinator:

- ALFA BLUE does **not** deliver sessions to children and has **no direct contact** with children;
- programmes are delivered by established youth-education NGOs and their schools, under the NGO's own safeguarding policy;
- ALFA BLUE does **not** receive or hold donations — sponsors fund the NGO partner directly.

**1.3** ALFA BLUE nonetheless influences children's safety through the partners it selects, the programmes it designs, the sponsors it introduces, and the data and images it handles. This policy covers those responsibilities.

**1.4** ALFA BLUE has zero tolerance of child abuse, exploitation and neglect. Where this policy and a partner's policy or local law differ, the higher standard of protection applies.

## 2. Scope

**2.1** This policy applies to:

- **ALFA BLUE Representatives** — its members, officers, employees, consultants and contractors;
- **Sponsors** — corporate, CSR, foundation and individual sponsors introduced by ALFA BLUE, when they take part in programme activities, events or communications;
- **Partners** — NGOs, schools and curriculum or platform providers, through the minimum standards in Section 7 and the partner agreement.

**2.2** It covers programme design, partner selection, sponsor introductions, attendance at partner-hosted events, and any handling of children's data, images or stories.

**2.3** Direct work with children is governed by the delivering NGO's safeguarding policy. ALFA BLUE checks that this policy meets the standards in Section 7 before any programme starts.

**2.4** Breach of this policy may lead to termination of employment, contract or partnership, and referral to the authorities.

## 3. Legal and Normative Framework

**3.1** This policy draws on the international standards below. Before supporting a programme in a new country, ALFA BLUE confirms with its NGO partner, and where needed with qualified counsel, the applicable local requirements.

| Framework | Relevance to ALFA BLUE |
| --- | --- |
| UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (UNCRC) | Foundation of children's rights to protection, participation and privacy |
| Keeping Children Safe — International Child Safeguarding Standards | Benchmark used by NGOs and sponsors, including for organisations that work with children only through partners |
| IASC Core Principles on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) | Standards expected by UN agencies and international funders |
| EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) | Benchmarks for handling children's personal data and for online learning platforms |
| Host-country child protection and data protection law | For example, in Egypt the Child Law (No. 12 of 1996, as amended) and Personal Data Protection Law (No. 151 of 2020); in China the Law on the Protection of Minors and the Personal Information Protection Law |

**3.2** Local mandatory reporting duties always take priority.

## 5. Guiding Principles

1. **Best interests of the child** — children's safety comes first in every programme, partner and sponsor decision.
2. **Zero tolerance** — abuse, exploitation and neglect are never acceptable, and every concern is acted on.
3. **Safe partners** — ALFA BLUE works only with delivery partners that meet recognised safeguarding standards.
4. **Clear responsibility** — each party knows its role: the NGO safeguards children in delivery; ALFA BLUE and sponsors protect children in everything they influence.
5. **Inclusion** — equal protection for every child, with attention to girls, children with disabilities and children in vulnerable situations.
6. **Confidentiality and dignity** — children's information and images are protected and used only with consent.
7. **Accountability** — ALFA BLUE acts on concerns, reports transparently to partners and sponsors, and learns from every incident.

## 7. Partner Due Diligence and Minimum Standards

**7.1** Partner due diligence is ALFA BLUE's main safeguarding control. Before introducing a delivery partner to a sponsor, or supporting a programme, ALFA BLUE checks that the partner meets the standards below, using the checklist in Annex A.

**7.2 Delivery partners (NGOs and schools) must have:**

- a written, board-approved safeguarding policy and a named safeguarding lead;
- safe recruitment of all educators and volunteers, including identity, references and police clearance where legally available;
- safeguarding training for everyone working with children, refreshed regularly;
- a code of conduct, supervised delivery settings, and no unsupervised one-to-one contact;
- parental or guardian consent for participation, data and images;
- a clear procedure for receiving concerns and referring them to the authorities.

**7.3 Online safety.** Where programmes use digital platforms or AI tools, the delivery partner and platform provider must ensure:

- age-appropriate content, with no advertising or data sale aimed at children;
- secure individual log-ins and moderated, programme-only communication channels;
- live online sessions only on approved platforms, with no private one-to-one contact between adults and children;
- AI tools used within the provider's age limits, under supervision, with no personal information entered;
- digital citizenship lessons on privacy, cyberbullying, grooming and seeking help.

**7.4 Partner agreements** include a safeguarding clause covering these standards, incident notification to ALFA BLUE, cooperation with any investigation, and ALFA BLUE's right to suspend or end the partnership, and to inform sponsors, after a serious safeguarding breach.

**7.5** Where a partner has gaps, ALFA BLUE agrees an improvement plan with deadlines and does not introduce sponsors until essential gaps are closed. Due diligence is repeated every two years, or sooner after a serious incident.

## 8. Code of Conduct for ALFA BLUE Representatives and Sponsors

**8.1** ALFA BLUE Representatives and sponsors do not work with children. They may occasionally attend partner-hosted events, such as programme launches, competitions or graduations. On those occasions they will:

- attend only with the delivery partner's agreement, and follow its safeguarding rules and briefing;
- stay accompanied by partner staff at all times, and never be alone with a child;
- treat every child with respect, and use appropriate language and behaviour;
- take photographs or video only as permitted in Section 9, and never on personal accounts;
- report any concern they see or hear to the partner's safeguarding lead and to ALFA BLUE within 24 hours.

**8.2** ALFA BLUE Representatives and sponsors will never:

- contact a child directly, in person or by phone, messaging apps or social media, or share personal contact details with a child;
- request children's personal data beyond the anonymised reporting agreed with the partner;
- give gifts, money or favours to individual children outside activities agreed with the partner;
- engage in any sexual activity or relationship with anyone under 18;
- ignore or keep secret any concern about a child's safety.

**8.3** ALFA BLUE Representatives sign the acknowledgement in Annex B. Sponsors accept this Code of Conduct as part of their sponsorship arrangement.

## 9. Children's Data, Images and Stories

**9.1** ALFA BLUE does not collect personal data from children. Delivery partners collect and hold participants' data under their own consent and data protection procedures.

| Topic | ALFA BLUE standard |
| --- | --- |
| Programme reporting | ALFA BLUE and sponsors receive aggregated or anonymised results only, such as numbers reached, completion rates and assessment gains, broken down by gender, age band and location |
| Personal data | Any exception, such as a named case study, requires specific written consent obtained by the partner from a parent or guardian, and a written data-sharing agreement |
| Images and video | Used only from material supplied or approved by the delivery partner, with consent confirmed; children shown with dignity and never identified by full name, school and location together |
| Stories and case studies | Names and identifying details changed unless the family gives specific consent; content reviewed by the partner before publication |
| Sponsor communications | Sponsors use children's images and stories only as supplied through ALFA BLUE or the partner, for the agreed purpose, and remove them if consent is withdrawn |
| Storage and retention | Any child-related material ALFA BLUE holds is kept on access-controlled company systems, never on personal devices, and deleted when no longer needed for the agreed purpose |
| Breaches | Any loss or misuse of children's data or images is reported to the delivery partner immediately and to regulators and families as required by law |

**9.2** Questions about personal data can be sent to [ReachUs@alfablueme.com](mailto:ReachUs@alfablueme.com). See also the ALFA BLUE Privacy Statement at [alfablueme.com/privacy](/privacy).

## 10. Reporting and Responding to Concerns

**10.1** The delivery partner leads the response to any concern about a child, because it has the relationship with the child, family and authorities. ALFA BLUE makes sure every concern it learns of reaches that partner and is followed up.

**Reporting route · partner-led, ALFA BLUE follows up**

Every concern goes first to the delivery partner, which acts and refers; ALFA BLUE is told within 24 hours and checks the follow-up.

**10.2 If an ALFA BLUE Representative or sponsor sees, hears or is told about a concern:**

- if a child is in immediate danger, alert partner staff at once and, if needed, emergency services;
- report the concern the same day to the partner's safeguarding lead;
- inform the ALFA BLUE Safeguarding Focal Point within 24 hours using [ReachUs@alfablueme.com](mailto:ReachUs@alfablueme.com) or \[phone\];
- if a child tells you directly, listen calmly, do not promise secrecy, do not question them further, and write down what was said in their words;
- never investigate or confront the person alleged to have caused harm.

**10.3 Concerns received by ALFA BLUE** from children, parents, educators or the public, including anonymous reports, are passed to the relevant partner's safeguarding lead within 24 hours, unless the concern involves that partner's leadership, in which case it goes directly to the authorities.

**10.4 ALFA BLUE follow-up.** The Focal Point records the concern, confirms the partner has acted, informs the Managing Director of serious cases, and reviews whether the partnership can continue safely. Sponsors linked to the programme are informed of serious incidents where appropriate, without identifying the child.

**10.5** Anyone who reports a concern in good faith is protected from retaliation, even if the concern is not confirmed.

## 11. Allegations Involving ALFA BLUE, Sponsors or Partners

**11.1** Any allegation that an ALFA BLUE Representative, a sponsor representative or a partner's staff member has harmed a child, or poses a risk, is reported to the Safeguarding Focal Point immediately. An allegation against the Focal Point goes to the Managing Director; one against the Managing Director goes to the named deputy or external adviser \[name\].

**11.2** ALFA BLUE will:

1. make sure the delivery partner has protected the child and arranged support;
2. make sure the matter is referred to the police or child protection authorities where a crime may have been committed;
3. remove any ALFA BLUE Representative or sponsor representative concerned from all programme activities while the matter is resolved;
4. cooperate fully with the authorities and the partner's investigation;
5. take disciplinary, contractual or partnership action based on the findings, including ending a sponsorship or partnership;
6. inform affected partners and sponsors of the outcome, as far as the law and the child's privacy allow.

## 12. Records, Monitoring and Review

**12.1** The Safeguarding Focal Point keeps confidential records of partner due diligence, concerns received, actions taken and outcomes. Records are stored securely, separately from general files, and shared only on a need-to-know basis.

**12.2** Each year the Focal Point reports to the Managing Director on concerns received, partner compliance and lessons learned, without identifying children.

**12.3** This policy is reviewed every 12 months, and sooner after a serious incident, a change in law, entry into a new country, or a partner or sponsor requirement.

**12.4** This policy is published on alfablueme.com and shared with every partner and sponsor at the start of the relationship.
