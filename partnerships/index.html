Who we work with

Partners we seek

NGOs

Community and youth organisations, local and international, with trusted relationships in the communities they serve and a mission to widen access to quality education.

What we offer together

A certified curriculum, educator training and programme coordination, so their reach becomes structured, high-quality learning.

Foundations & development organisations

Philanthropic foundations, development agencies and multilateral programmes focused on education, digital inclusion, youth employability or gender equity.

What we offer together

Programmes designed with clear objectives and quality standards from the outset, with transparent reporting.

CSR & ESG teams

Companies whose social-impact, ESG or community-investment strategy includes education, digital skills or financial literacy, often in the markets where they operate.

What we offer together

Credible, certified initiatives aligned with corporate commitments and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 4, Quality Education).

Schools & education authorities

Public and private schools, school networks and ministries or regional authorities seeking to introduce or strengthen digital and financial literacy.

What we offer together

Practical integration options (in-timetable, after-school or blended), teacher training and age-appropriate learning pathways.

Proposed pathway

From needs assessment to local delivery

  1. 01

    Needs assessment

    Understand the community, existing provision, infrastructure and learner needs.

  2. 02

    Programme design

    Select curriculum, delivery model, educator training and quality standards.

  3. 03

    Funding

    Structure sponsorship, philanthropic or institutional funding for the programme.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Implement through authorised partners, with coordination and quality oversight.

  5. 05

    Partner reporting

    Delivery partners report on their programmes directly to their own sponsors.

  6. 06

    Scale & refine

    Use evidence to improve the programme and extend it to new communities.

In cooperation with an AI learning-analytics partner

Needs assessment and progress tracking are supported by a K-12 education-technology partner based in Asia, whose patent-pending AI engine runs adaptive diagnostic assessments to identify each student's individual learning gaps and recommend personalised remediation. Its platform gives teachers, school leaders and parents clear, data-driven insight into progress, and is used by leading private schools.

This allows delivery partners to establish a reliable baseline before a programme starts. Delivery partners can use the platform's built-in assessments to follow learners' progress.
