Safeguarding

Safeguarding & Child Protection

Children's safety comes first in every programme, partner and sponsor decision we make.

Our commitment

Zero tolerance of abuse, exploitation and neglect

ALFA BLUE has zero tolerance of abuse, exploitation and neglect. Children's safety comes first in every programme, partner and sponsor decision.

Our role

ALFA BLUE does not work directly with children. Programmes are delivered by established NGO partners under their own safeguarding policies, which must meet our minimum standards.

How we protect children

Safeguards built into every partnership

Partner due diligence

We check every delivery partner's safeguarding policy, staff vetting, training and reporting procedures before any collaboration.

Online safety

Digital platforms must be age-appropriate, secure and free of advertising aimed at children.

Data and images

We receive only anonymised programme information; images are used only with consent obtained by the partner.

Code of conduct

Our team and sponsors attending partner events follow strict conduct rules and are always accompanied by partner staff.

Raising a concern

If you have a concern about a child in a programme we support, please contact our Safeguarding Focal Point at ReachUs@alfablueme.com. Concerns are passed to the delivering partner's safeguarding lead within 24 hours and treated confidentially.
